MLKit region profile

Allocated region footprint, including page headers and earlier-page slack; finite regions show reserved stack space. This is neither RSS nor reachable data. Thread attribution follows lifetime ownership. Worker identity is the owner's execution stream at sampling time, not the origin of allocations.

Finite regions reserve space on the ML stack; infinite regions use region pages and may contain large objects.

The largest regions by summed sampled size are retained; Other combines the rest. Bands run from smallest to largest, bottom to top, with Other at the bottom. The legend follows the graph from top to bottom. Colors identify bindings. Shared and persistent/global regions follow lifetime ownership in filtered views. CPU means OS logical processor at the safe point, where available; an execution stream is not a physical core.
